Play prescription

🩺 Everyday care • English

Play is how young brains grow — 15 focused minutes with you beats hours of passive screen time. Follow the band for your child's age.

🧸 0–6 months
  1. Talk, sing and smile back at every coo.
  2. Tummy time 2–3 times a day while awake.
  3. Show high-contrast pictures and rattles.
  4. No screen time — your face is the best toy.
🧸 6–12 months
  1. Name objects during daily routines.
  2. Peek-a-boo, clapping and stacking cups.
  3. Let the baby bang, drop and explore safely.
  4. Read a picture book daily, even briefly.
🧸 1–3 years
  1. Pretend play: kitchen set, dolls, toy phone.
  2. Scribbling with crayons, sorting shapes.
  3. Simple instructions: 'bring the ball'.
  4. Outdoor play + sand/water play daily.
🧸 3–6 years
  1. Stories and rhymes; ask 'what next?'.
  2. Drawing, puzzles, building blocks.
  3. Play with other children; take turns.
  4. Limit screens to 1 hour of quality content.
🧸 6 years+
  1. Sports + 60 min active play daily.
  2. Reading habit: 15 min together.
  3. Chores build responsibility.
  4. Discuss online safety for any device use.
