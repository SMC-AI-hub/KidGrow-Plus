Vomiting — home care

🩺 Everyday care • English

Most vomiting comes from stomach viruses and stops within 24–48 hours. Small, frequent sips prevent dehydration far better than big glasses.

WHAT TO DO AT HOME
  1. Wait 15–20 minutes after a vomit, then restart with small sips of ORS or water.
  2. Give 5–10 mL every 5–10 minutes; increase slowly if it stays down.
  3. Continue breastfeeding; restart light feeds (khichdi, curd rice, banana) once vomiting eases.
  4. Avoid fried, spicy food and full milk bottles for a day.
  5. Check urine: passing urine every 6–8 hours means hydration is okay.
AVOID THESE MISTAKES
  • Don't force big glasses of water or juice right after vomiting — it triggers more.
  • Don't give anti-vomiting or antibiotic syrups without advice.
  • Don't lay a vomiting baby flat on the back — keep the head raised or side-lying.
WHAT TO EXPECT
Vomiting usually slows within 12–24 hours; loose stools may follow for a few days as the virus clears.
🚨 WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR
Return immediately: blood or green colour in vomit, severe tummy pain, no urine >8 hours, unusually sleepy, vomiting >24 hours.
