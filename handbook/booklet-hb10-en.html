Handbook 10 — Mother's food, rest & mood

📖 Infant Feeding Handbook • English
© 2026 NeoKid Care • All rights reserved

A fed, rested, supported mother breastfeeds longer. Add a meal + snack daily, share chores, sleep when baby sleeps — and treat low mood as illness, not failure.

WHAT TO DO AT HOME
  1. Eat one extra meal + snack: dal, egg/curd/fish, greens, fruit, plus iodized salt.
  2. Continue iron + calcium tablets; drink fluids to thirst — forcing litres doesn't raise supply.
  3. No food bans while nursing — restrict a food only if baby clearly reacts every time.
  4. Sleep when baby sleeps; share cooking, night shifts, and older children with family.
  5. Short walks, sun, and company lift mood — isolation worsens it.
  6. Plan contraception before 6 weeks — fertility can return before the first period.
AVOID THESE MISTAKES
  • Don't crash-diet — it saps energy and supply; eat adequately.
  • Don't use alcohol, tobacco, or gutka — quit with help; cap tea/coffee at ~2 cups.
  • Don't suffer silently — sadness beyond 2 weeks or harm thoughts need care this week.
WHAT TO EXPECT
Tearful overwhelmed days 3–10 ('blues') pass with rest and support. True appetite and energy return gradually over weeks.
🚨 WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR
Urgent: can't bond or feels resentful toward baby, can't function, severe anxiety/panic, or any thoughts of harm — to self or baby — need immediate care.
