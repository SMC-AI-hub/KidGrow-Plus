Chapter 1 — Introduction to infant nutrition

👪 PARENT. Food in the first 2 years builds the brain, body defences and growth that last a lifetime. The plan is simple: only your milk for 6 months → family foods from 6 months → your milk + foods till 2 years. This book shows you exactly how, step by step.

Why infancy nutrition decides lifelong health

Growth velocity never again matches the first year (birth weight triples by 12 months; brain reaches ~75% of adult size by age 2). Undernutrition here causes stunting, weak immunity and lost learning potential — largely irreversible after age 2. Overnutrition sets obesity pathways. Feeding is therefore treatment, not just care.

One size never fits all

GroupDefinitionFeeding headline
Healthy term≥37 weeksExclusive breastfeeding; foods at 6 mo

Role of the healthcare team

Protect (no routine separation/supplementation), promote (counsel every contact), support (positioning, expression, return-to-work plans) — and prescribe precisely when human milk is unavailable. Document every feeding plan; review weight before changing anything.

⭐ Chapter takeaways. First 2 years = once-in-a-lifetime window · breastmilk first, always · term and preterm pathways differ from day 1 · the growth curve, not the recipe, judges feeding.

Mini-glossary

Colostrum — first thick yellow milk · EBM — expressed breast milk · KMC — kangaroo mother care · LBW/VLBW/ELBW — <2500/<1500/<1000 g · Fortifier — nutrient booster added to human milk (NICU only) · Responsive feeding — feeding to cues, not clock.

Chapter 2 — Breast milk: composition & benefits

👪 PARENT. Your milk is alive: it changes from feed to feed and month to month, always matching your baby. No factory can copy its infection-fighting and brain-building parts — that is why every drop counts, including the very first yellow drops.

Foremilk / hindmilk — the honest version

Milk gradually gets fattier during a feed — there is no sharp switch. Practical rule: finish the first breast first instead of clock-timing sides. Green frothy stools + fussiness can mean too-frequent switching (lactose overload) — fix by longer one-side feeds.

🩺 Donor milk note. Pasteurized donor human milk (regulated milk bank) is second choice after MOM for preterm/LBW (WHO 2022). Informal milk sharing is discouraged (infection/lifestyle risks). Fortification of MOM/PDHM for <32 wk/<1500 g — see Chapter 6.
