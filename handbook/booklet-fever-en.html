Fever — home care

🩺 Everyday care • English
© 2026 KidGrow Pro • All rights reserved

Fever (100.4°F / 38°C or more) is the body's defence against infection. Most childhood fevers are viral and settle in 2–3 days. How the child behaves matters more than the thermometer number.

DOSE GUIDE
⚖️ Paracetamol 10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 hours (max 4 doses in 24 hours). Your child's exact dose is computed on the app handout — confirm with your doctor.
WHAT TO DO AT HOME
  1. Fever is the body's way of fighting infection. Treat the child, not just the number.
  2. Give paracetamol (dose as prescribed) if the child is uncomfortable or fever is above 100.4°F (38°C).
  3. Paracetamol 10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 hours; maximum 4 doses in 24 hours.
  4. Offer plenty of fluids — water, breastmilk, soups, ORS.
  5. Dress lightly; do not over-wrap or give a cold-water bath.
  6. Check temperature every 4–6 hours and note it down.
  7. Antibiotics are not needed for most viral fevers.
AVOID THESE MISTAKES
  • Don't over-wrap the child or sponge with cold water — shivering pushes fever higher.
  • Don't give aspirin, ibuprofen, or adult cold-and-flu medicines without advice.
  • Don't wake a sleeping, comfortable child just to check the temperature.
WHAT TO EXPECT
Fever often climbs in the evening for 2–3 days, then fades. Appetite returns last — keep fluids and light feeds going meanwhile.
🚨 WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR
Return immediately: fits/seizure, unusually sleepy/limp, can't drink or breastfeed, vomits everything, breathing difficulty, rash with fever, fever >3 days, baby <3 months with fever.
