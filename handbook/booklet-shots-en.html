After vaccination

🩺 Everyday care • English

Mild fever, pain, or a small lump after vaccines means the immune system is learning — a good sign. The BCG spot forms a tiny scar over weeks; that is normal too.

WHAT TO DO AT HOME
  1. Mild fever, pain or swelling for 1–2 days is common and harmless.
  2. Give paracetamol only if the child is uncomfortable (dose as prescribed).
  3. Apply a clean cool cloth on a sore injection site.
  4. Continue normal feeds; let the child play.
  5. Note the next due date on the vaccine card — do not miss it.
AVOID THESE MISTAKES
  • Don't rub or press the injection site hard, or apply hot fomentation.
  • Don't give paracetamol routinely 'to prevent fever' — only if the child is uncomfortable.
  • Don't skip the next dose just because of mild fever this time.
WHAT TO EXPECT
Fever or soreness peaks in the first 24 hours and settles by day 2. A BCG lump takes 3–6 weeks to become a tiny scar.
🚨 WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR
Return immediately: crying >3 hours inconsolably, seizure, floppy/unresponsive episode, breathing difficulty, fast-spreading swelling.
