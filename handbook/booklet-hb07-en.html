Handbook 7 — Formula: safe & responsive

📖 Infant Feeding Handbook • English

When formula is needed, safety is everything: powder isn't sterile, ratios must be exact, and bottles are paced — baby controls the feed, never propped.

WHAT TO DO AT HOME
  1. Doctor chooses the type (infant vs follow-up vs special) — never self-switch for fussiness.
  2. Sterilize bottle, teat, scoop; wash hands every time.
  3. Boil water, cool under 30 min — mix with water at 70°C or hotter to kill germs.
  4. Water first, then exact level scoops with the enclosed scoop — no heaping, no guessing.
  5. Cool to lukewarm, wrist-test, feed within 2 hours; batch bottles: chill fast, use in 24 h.
  6. Feed upright, bottle flat, pause and burp, stop at fullness cues.
AVOID THESE MISTAKES
  • Don't microwave formula — hot spots burn; warm in warm water instead.
  • Don't dilute or concentrate — weak starves, strong strains kidneys.
  • Don't prop the bottle, feed flat, add cereal, or save leftovers.
WHAT TO EXPECT
Formula stools are firmer and less frequent than breastfed — true constipation means hard dry pellets. Small possets with good gain are normal.
🚨 WHEN TO SEE YOUR DOCTOR
Doctor review: blood or mucus in stools, eczema with gut symptoms, poor growth, forceful vomiting, or any formula-prep mistake with a sick baby.
